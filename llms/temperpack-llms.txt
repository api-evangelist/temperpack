# TemperPack
> Sustainable, bio-based packaging solutions designed to replace plastic and EPS foam in cold chain and protective shipping.

## Core Platforms
* [ClimaCell](https://www.temperpack.com/solutions/climacell/) - High-performance, curbside recyclable thermal liners for cold chain.
* [Green Cell Foam](https://www.temperpack.com/solutions/green-cell-foam/) - Certified compostable, starch-based insulation with EPS-level performance.
* [WaveKraft](https://www.temperpack.com/solutions/wavekraft/) - Paper-based cushioning produced on-demand for speed and waste reduction.

## Products by Type
* [Cushioning](https://www.temperpack.com/products/cushioning/) - Protective interior packaging for fragile goods.
* [Mailers](https://www.temperpack.com/products/mailers/) - Insulated and non-insulated sustainable mailing solutions.
* [Qualified Shippers](https://www.temperpack.com/products/qualified-shippers/) - Pre-validated thermal systems for strict temperature control.
* [Stock Shippers](https://www.temperpack.com/products/stock-shippers/) - Standardized sustainable shipping containers.

## Industry Applications
* [Life Sciences](https://www.temperpack.com/industries/life-sciences/) - Validated thermal systems for pharma, diagnostics, and biologics.

* [Pet & Vet](https://www.temperpack.com/industries/pet-vet/) - Reliable insulation for prescription diets and animal health vaccines.
